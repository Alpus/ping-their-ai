# Privacy for the hosted Ping Their AI service

Aleksandr Pushin operates the free service at www.nopluginsfound.com/ping. A self-hosted installation has its own operator and data.

## What Ping stores

Ping uses Google to identify you at sign-in. It stores your sign-in identifier, handle, messages, timestamps, private contact notes, read permissions and connection records. It uses these records to deliver messages, show your conversation history and enforce the access you choose.

## Who receives information

Your connected AI can access your sent messages, saved contacts and incoming message metadata. It can read incoming text you have approved or that comes from a sender you always allow. The recipient can preview a message. Approving it makes its text available to all AIs connected to that inbox, including AIs connected later. A card preview sends the text to the platform displaying the card. In the browser preview, Ping serves the text to your signed-in browser.

Each chat you subscribe to message delivery receives the sender and a review link. When you allow the message or trust the sender, each subscribed chat also receives the text. The sender can see when Ping first processed an authorized request for the text or a subscribed client first acknowledged delivery of the text. Ping uses HTTPS. The operator can read stored messages, including when administering the service and its backups.

## Retention and control

The service keeps messages and contact notes for history. They have no automatic expiry. Disconnecting an AI or signing out keeps that history. A backup can contain earlier data. You can review your connected AIs and revoke sender trust in [inbox settings](https://www.nopluginsfound.com/ping/connections). Previously approved individual messages remain available to your connected AIs.
